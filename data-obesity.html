An Application of the Burger with Swivel™ Model

Your data hoard
is not an asset.
It is a liability
accruing interest.

Every untriaged terabyte is breach surface, regulatory exposure, discovery cost, and storage debt — compounding daily. The discipline is no longer to collect more. It is to hold only what regulation requires, and what the business can monetize. Everything else must be defensibly destroyed.

The Thesis
Data is the focus at the top of the Burger. It enters the stack, flows through Governance, Controls, and Analytics & AI, and exits as Outcomes. Obesity at the focus point distorts every layer below it. Fix the entry. The stack performs.
01 · The Anatomy

The Burger. With Swivel.

Reading the Burger

Data is the focus at the top — what you actually hold. It flows through the stack and exits at the bottom as Outcomes. Every layer in between is a discipline the data must pass through to earn its keep.

The Swivel means the model rotates. It applies to any business unit, region, system, or data class — but the order of operations never changes. Core first. Outcomes last.

Core · 00 Data Real Estate — classify, rationalise
First · 01 Governance & Policy
Second · 02 Controls & Protections
Third · 03 Business Alignment — Analytics & AI
Top · 04 Outcomes — the deliverable
02 · The Diagnosis

Five Core defects. One pathology.

S/01
Untriaged growth
Storage compounds 30–50% YoY with no classification gate at ingest.
S/02
Dark data dominance
60–80% of the corpus is unknown, unowned, unsearched, untouched.
S/03
Orphaned datasets
Departed teams, deprecated systems, legacy migrations — still live in production.
S/04
Redundant copies
The same record exists in 5–11 stores. No canonical source of truth.
S/05
Indefinite by default
"Keep forever" is the unwritten policy. Deletion has no owner.
03 · The Liability Math
Held Data =
Breach Surface × Regulatory Exposure
× Discovery Cost × Storage TCO

Every variable scales with volume. None of them scale with the value of the data held. The only term the CIO controls is the multiplicand on the left.

04 · The Doctrine, By Layer

Read the Burger top down.

Core00
Data Real EstateKYD + KYP
Survey the estate. Classify every parcel. Rationalise ruthlessly. Decide — by policy, not preference — what to keep and what to defensibly destroy. The Core is where obesity begins, and where it must end.

First
Layer01
Governance & PolicyRegulation · Retention · Consent · Lineage
Every dataset bound to a retention obligation — HIPAA, HITRUST, GDPR, SOX, DPDP, contract. No obligation, no right to hold. Lineage and consent are first-class metadata, not afterthoughts.
"Holding is permission,
not default."
Second
Layer02
Controls & ProtectionsEncryption · IAM · DLP · Backup
Protect the lean corpus, not the bloated one. Controls scale with risk class, not raw volume. Backups follow retention policy, not vendor defaults.
"Protect what
survives the cull."
Third
Layer03
Business AlignmentBI · Analytics · AI
Value creation flows downstream of governance — never upstream. Ungoverned data is not a moat; it is exposure. Monetize only what is classified, retained on purpose, and consented.
"Monetize the governed.
Refuse the rest."
Top
Layer04
OutcomesThe Deliverable
Measurable shrinkage of the liability surface. Demonstrable analytics velocity. Clean audit trails. Board-grade evidence of disciplined stewardship.
"Lean by design.
Defensible by default."
